# Future Ideas

Ideas worth revisiting once core systems are stable. Not prioritized.

## Gacha / Reward System
- **Combat/exploration pull earning** — pulls earnable through combat milestones (first kill, N kills) and exploration milestones (rooms discovered, POIs interacted), supplementing the streak-based weekly pull cadence.

## Exploration Engagement
- **Class-specific perception** — different characters notice different things in the same room based on class/background (rogue sees trip wire, wizard senses magical residue). Drives party coordination organically.
- **Party visibility feed** — lightweight ticker showing what party members are actively doing ("Kira is examining the bookshelf"). Makes the shared space feel real without mechanical overhead.
- **Collaborative puzzles** — POIs that require multiple characters to interact in sequence. Needs story integration and data model work (`PoiState` currently has no per-character interaction tracking).
- **Environmental pressure** — time-sensitive exploration elements unrelated to combat (torches burning, sounds getting closer). Creates urgency and coordination incentive.

## Social / Meta
- **Session highlight reel** — post-session card showing a player's actions, discoveries, and AI-picked best narrative moment.
- **Chaos score** — AI tags responses where the player did something genuinely unexpected; tracked per character over time.
